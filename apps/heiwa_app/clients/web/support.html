Support · Help & Disclosure

How to get help. How to report. What to expect.

Heiwa is local-first software. Support runs through the public source tree, the documentation site, and a small set of disclosure channels. Response-time fiducials are honest, not aspirational.

03 · Disclosure Policy

COORDINATED · PRIVATE FIRST
REPORT INCLUDES
  • Affected component (runtime, CLI, web shell, evidence)
  • Heiwa version · heiwa --version
  • Reproduction steps or proof-of-concept
  • Suspected impact · severity estimate
  • Disclosure preferences · embargo window
WE COMMIT TO
  • Acknowledge within 24h
  • Triage within 72h
  • Coordinate a disclosure window — default 30 days
  • Credit researchers in SECURITY.md unless asked not to
  • Publish a postmortem in docs/security/ after fix lands

05 · What This Channel Does Not Cover

PROVIDER BOUNDARIES
PROVIDER RUNTIMES

Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Antigravity, Ollama, and OpenRouter own their inference, auth, quotas, and model inventory. Heiwa wraps; it does not adjudicate provider behaviour.

Provider issues route to the provider's own support.

OPERATOR DATA

Memory, sessions, evidence, and provider secrets live on the operator's machine. Heiwa support cannot read, recover, or restore that data — by design.

Backups remain the operator's responsibility.
